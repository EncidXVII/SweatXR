A fitness watch for every VR game

FitnessVR is a free VR fitness tracker for SteamVR. Glance at your wrist in any game to see calories burned, active minutes and heart rate, with a daily goal ring. No mods, no account, and your data stays on your computer.

Download for Windows Linux and Steam Frame

Free and open source (GPL-3.0). Windows x64, Linux x64, Linux arm64. Version 1.0.0.

Built for how you play

Every SteamVR game

No mods or per-game support. Rhythm, boxing, dance, sports and flat-to-VR ports all count.

Calories per game

See which game burns the most, with daily goals, streaks, and week and 30-day history.

Heart rate straps

Pair any Bluetooth LE heart rate strap for better estimates, with live bpm and zone on your wrist.

No strap? Still counts

Effort is estimated from head and hand movement, then calibrated to you after 20 minutes with a strap.

Stays out of the way

The watch dims when you look away, brightens on a glance and hides near your face. Under 0.1 ms per redraw.

Private and portable

No account, cloud or telemetry. Export TCX for your fitness apps, or CSV with every second.

In VR and on your desktop

Install in 3 steps

  1. Download the archive for your system from the latest release and check it against its .sha256 file.
  2. Windows: unpack the zip and keep the folder. Linux or Steam Frame (Developer Mode): make the AppImage executable, or unpack the tar.gz and run ./install.sh.
  3. Start SteamVR and run fitvr once. It registers itself and starts with SteamVR from then on.

Works with Valve Index, Steam Frame, HTC Vive, Pimax, Bigscreen Beyond, Windows Mixed Reality, and Meta Quest through Steam Link, Virtual Desktop or a Link cable.

FAQ

How many calories does VR gaming burn?

It depends on the game and on you. Rhythm and boxing games often reach the effort of brisk walking or jogging, while seated games burn little more than resting. FitnessVR measures your own sessions, so you see the real number for each game.

Does FitnessVR work with Meta Quest?

Yes, when the Quest plays SteamVR games from a PC through Steam Link, Virtual Desktop or a Link cable. Standalone Quest games are not tracked.

Does it work on Steam Frame?

Yes. The Linux arm64 build runs natively on the headset in Developer Mode, and the PC build works when streaming.

Do I need a heart rate monitor?

No. Without one, calories are estimated from head and hand movement. A Bluetooth LE heart rate strap improves the estimate and calibrates the movement model to you.

Does it slow down my games?

No. It reads tracking from SteamVR and draws a small overlay in about 0.1 ms; it never touches game files.

Is FitnessVR free?

Yes. It is free and open source under GPL-3.0, with no account, ads or subscription.

Calorie numbers are estimates; FitnessVR is not a medical device.
