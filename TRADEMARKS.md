# Names and logos

The name **SweatXR** and its logo (the mark in `packaging/brand`) identify this project and the builds its author,
[EncidXVII](https://github.com/EncidXVII), publishes. They are not covered by the software license: the [GPL](LICENSE) lets you use, change
and share the code, and [NOTICE](NOTICE) (section 7(e) of the license) keeps the name and logo out of that grant.
They are used as trademarks; they are not registered.

## Fine without asking

- Saying that your video, article, guide or tool is about SweatXR, works with SweatXR or is based on SweatXR.
- Sharing the unmodified release files under their own name.
- Using the logo next to the name to point at this project (a link, a review, a list of tools).
- Packaging the unmodified program for a distribution or a store listing that names the author and links here.

## Not allowed

- Giving a changed version the name SweatXR, a name easily mistaken for it, or its logo. A fork needs its own name and
  icon, and says "based on SweatXR by EncidXVII" (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)).
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests the author made, checked or endorses something they did not.
- Selling merchandise or registering domains, store pages or social accounts under the name.

For anything else, open an issue and ask.
